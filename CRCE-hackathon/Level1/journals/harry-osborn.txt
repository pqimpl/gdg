Harry Osborn — Engineering Notes
--------------------------------

Found an old backup machine while going through some of the
company's archived equipment.

The credential record is strange.

The digest is represented using hexadecimal characters only.

It is exactly 32 characters long.

That means it represents 128 bits of data.

Dad always hated these old systems. He called their password
protection "obsolete the day it shipped."

I asked Otto what he thought.

He laughed and wrote something in the margin:

    "128-bit Message Digest"

He refused to explain further.
