Aunt May — Personal Notes
-------------------------

Peter came home late again.

He was complaining about the backup system and how old everything
was. Apparently the machine still uses one of those ridiculous
legacy password conventions.

I asked him what that meant.

He said the old credentials were usually made from:

    one lowercase word
    followed by exactly two digits

He said people were supposed to avoid birthdays, names and anything
easy to guess.

I told him that sounded like something Otto would have designed.

He disagreed.

He then left to get groceries.

P.S. Peter insists that keeping passwords simple "for old systems"
does not make them good passwords.
