Otto Octavius — Research Log
----------------------------

Legacy systems are fascinating.

One particular authentication mechanism keeps appearing in
the old laboratory equipment.

The digest size is 128 bits.

The implementation belongs to the family commonly referred to as:

    Message-Digest Algorithm 5

It was never designed to resist modern password cracking.

Given enough candidate plaintexts, recovering the original value
is a matter of comparing generated digests against the stored one.

An amusingly primitive system.

Still useful for understanding how badly designed authentication
systems fail.
